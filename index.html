Chhand Generator

Usage Guide ▼

Pattern Template Logic: Map position numbers to the respective inputs across pairs.

Example Pattern: 1 2 2 1 or 1 2

Swar Rules: Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni

  • , → Komal (Re, Ga, Dha, Ni only)
  • ' → Tivra (Ma only)
  • * → Taar Saptak (Higher)
  • . → Mandra Saptak (Lower)

Multiple Notes per Value: Separate notes with spaces (e.g., Re, Ma'). Leave blank or omit numbers to treat as an unentered note.
